📖 Mission Briefing — What This Simulation Is Doing

MarsNet-RL demonstrates why satellite constellations orbiting Mars need to protect themselves: a radio signal takes up to ~20 minutes each way between Earth and Mars, so ground control physically cannot approve a collision-avoidance maneuver in time. Each satellite here is an independent reinforcement-learning agent that senses nearby debris, moons, and other satellites, uses a predictive engine to forecast conflicts before they happen, and decides — on its own, in real time — whether to thrust, hold position, or return to its assigned orbit, all while managing a limited fuel (Delta-V) budget.
